## [1.0.0] - 2026-09-05

### Added

- Two-level category and Prompt context menus for editable fields.
- Plain-text insertion at the current caret or selection.
- Local-first storage with compressed per-category Chrome Sync.
- Durable retries, quota visibility, deletion tombstones, and offline conflict handling.
- Options page for category and Prompt management.
- Automated static, mock-background, and real-Chrome smoke checks.
